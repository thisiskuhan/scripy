Privacy & Local Files

Scripy is an offline screenplay editor. This edition does not require an account or internet connection. Sign-in and cloud storage are disabled.

Your screenplays

Drafts are kept on your device. Opening, saving, importing, and exporting operate on your local workspace and the files you select. The application does not upload your screenplays to a cloud service.

Local recovery

Drafts, recovery snapshots, recent-document references, appearance, and panel preferences are stored in the application profile on this device. The browser development preview uses a separate browser database. Recent files are drawn from that local workspace, not a scan of your computer.

Local files and recovery databases are not encrypted by Scripy; other people with access to your device or profile may be able to inspect them. Keep independent backups before clearing site data or an app profile.

Removing local data

You can remove files through your operating system's file manager. Clearing the desktop app profile or browser site data removes local recovery and preferences; it does not delete separately saved screenplay files. Earlier account-scoped browser data is retained without being merged into the offline workspace.

External services

No application telemetry, advertising, AI processing, Google sign-in, or Drive requests are active in this edition. Downloading an installer from a release-hosting website is subject to that website's own privacy policy.
